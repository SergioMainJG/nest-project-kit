## What the license requires

Every copy or substantial portion of the software must keep:

1. The copyright notice: `Copyright (c) 2026 Sergio Elias Arce Roldán`.
2. The MIT permission notice, that is, the full text of [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

If you redistribute the **built bundle** (`dist/main.cjs`), also keep `dist/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`. The bundle contains third-party code whose licenses (MIT, ISC, BSD-3-Clause, 0BSD and Apache-2.0) require their notices to travel with it. The build creates that file automatically.

This obligation does not apply to the projects the tool generates: generated code is yours to license however you want (see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md), section 3).

## Recommendations for modified versions

These are courtesies rather than legal conditions, but they protect both you and the users of your version:

- **Use a different name.** Publish forks under your own npm scope or name, not `@sergioar/nest-project-kit`, so users can tell the versions apart.
- **Say what you changed.** Add a line under the copyright notice in LICENSE.md, for example `Portions Copyright (c) <year> <your name>`, or keep a changelog of your modifications.
- **Keep NOTICE.md.** It records that the tool is unofficial and unaffiliated with NestJS, and where the templates come from. Add your own notices to it rather than removing the existing ones.
- **Do not imply endorsement.** Do not use the original author's name, or the NestJS name and logo, to suggest that they sponsor or approve your version.

## When you change the templates

- Generated projects pull dependencies from npm. If you add a new dependency to the templates (`src/generator/versions.ts`), check that its license allows the uses your users expect, and mention any non-permissive or missing license in NOTICE.md, as section 5 does for `nestjs-platform-bun`.
- If you copy code from another project into the templates, its license applies to that code, and to the code generated from it, unless the author permits otherwise. Keep its notice next to the copied code, and do not extend the permission in NOTICE.md, section 3, to code you are not entitled to relicense.
